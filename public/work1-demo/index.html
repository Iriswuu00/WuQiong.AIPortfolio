About Us

Zhejiang Jinke Talking Tom Culture Industry Co., Ltd. was established in June 2007 and listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange GEM in May 2015 (Stock Code: 300459). The company is a full-spectrum IP operator centered around the "Talking Tom & Friends" IP, integrating online and offline, entertainment and education, derivatives and theme parks. The company owns the globally renowned "Talking Tom & Friends" IP, driving mobile applications, animation, and film businesses online, while expanding into IP derivatives, licensing, and Talking Tom family parks offline. In recent years, the company has embraced AI and other cutting-edge technologies to continuously innovate interactive scenarios, making strides in family education and cultural entertainment to build an internationally influential cultural brand. To date, the company's products have accumulated over 24 billion downloads worldwide, with peak monthly active users reaching 470 million. The "Talking Tom & Friends" animated series has garnered over 60 billion views globally, covering more than 200 countries and regions, establishing a massive user base and extensive brand influence worldwide.

Talking Tom & Friends

Vibrant companions for every moment of growth

Talking Tom & Friends is a globally renowned virtual IP character ensemble created by Outfit7. Since the release of its first app in 2010, this joyful family has accompanied hundreds of millions of users worldwide. The core family members include the cheerful Talking Tom, the sweet Angela, the loyal Hank, the studious Ben, the quirky Becca, and the mischievous Ginger. Each character has a distinct personality and unique charm. Empowered by AI technology, they are stepping into real life in brand-new interactive forms, bringing warmer companionship experiences to children and families.
