This Policy applies to the products and services we provide through websites, applications, mini-programs, smart hardware, third-party platforms via Software Development Kits (SDKs), Application Programming Interfaces (APIs), and future innovative formats enabled by technological advancements (collectively, "Services" or "AI Tom Bud") (including, but not limited to, platforms centered on "AI interactive dialogue" and other functionalities).

Definitions

In this Policy:

  • Personal Information: Information recorded electronically or otherwise that can identify a specific individual or reflect their activities, either independently or in combination with other data.
  • Smart Hardware Devices: Non-standard computing devices produced by manufacturers with human-machine interfaces and wireless networking capabilities.
  • Application: Mobile applications (including mobile clients, mini-programs, and WeChat mini-programs) developed by us to help users remotely control smart devices and connect to our cloud platform.

1. Collection and Use of Personal Information

We adhere to principles of legality, fairness, necessity, and integrity when collecting and using your Personal Information for the purposes below.

1.1. Overview of Personal Information Collection and Use

The core functionality of AI Tom Bud is human-machine interactive dialogue. To provide additional services such as device binding, device management, usage statistics, and dialogue summaries, we require basic Personal Information necessary for these functionalities, unless otherwise mandated by laws or regulations. If you refuse to provide such information, we may be unable to deliver the corresponding services. Note: If you provide another individual’s Personal Information, ensure you have obtained their consent; otherwise, you assume liability for any consequences.

1.1.1. Registration/Login

To comply with legal requirements and provide registration/login services, you must provide a mobile number or email address. We will send a verification code to confirm your identity.

If you decline to provide this information, we may be unable to offer AI interactive dialogue and other functionalities. Apple ID and Google Account login options are also available. When using these methods, you must bind a mobile number or email for identity verification.

1.1.2. Device Binding

To bind the application to a hardware device, we may request permissions for location, Bluetooth, camera, and WLAN access. During the device network configuration and binding process, we collect your current Wi-Fi network information (including SSID, i.e., network name, and BSSID, i.e., network identifier) to help the smart hardware device connect to the same Wi-Fi network as your phone in order to complete device network configuration and binding. We also collect:

  • Wi-Fi status, location data (to retrieve Wi-Fi networks), Wi-Fi network details, device connection status, hardware/device models, operating system, and IP address.

This information is uploaded to our servers to prevent unauthorized access and enhance security. You may unbind devices via the "Unbind" feature.

1.1.3. Device Management

After binding a device, you can manage functionalities (e.g., renaming devices, authorizing music features) through the application. Your settings and data are uploaded to our servers for device control.

1.1.4. Interactive Dialogue

We use generative AI models to provide chat and interaction services. When using the chat function, we automatically receive and record audio inputs (e.g., voice conversations, recordings) to deliver intelligent responses.

For weather inquiries, we use approximate location data to provide localized weather updates.

Under applicable laws, we may de-identify/anonymize contextual interaction data to avoid identifying individuals, ensuring privacy while improving service quality and user experience.

If your inputs include others’ Personal Information, ensure you have obtained their consent to prevent unauthorized disclosure.

1.1.5. Usage Statistics and Summaries

To ensure service security and quality, we record your interaction times, chat logs (including inputs, system responses, and derived dialogue themes) and display them as bar charts or summaries in the app.

1.1.6. Operations and Security

To maintain secure and efficient operations, we collect:

Log data (user ID, request timestamps), usage details (client/server timestamps, device type, network info), device info (model), and network data (connection type, status, quality, Wi-Fi network name SSID, Wi-Fi network identifier BSSID).

This data assists in troubleshooting service anomalies.

1.1.7. Sensor Information

When you use the camera scanning feature, we access the device's accelerometer sensor data to detect device orientation changes, ensuring the correct orientation of the camera preview and captured content. This sensor data is processed locally on your device only while you are using the camera feature, and is not uploaded to our servers or used for any other purpose.

1.2. Rules for Information Use

We use collected information only for the purposes stated in this Policy. If we use your Personal Information beyond the original scope, we will notify and obtain your explicit consent. We apply de-identification measures where appropriate.

Under applicable laws, we may collect/use Personal Information without consent in the following cases:

  • 1. To fulfill contractual or legal obligations;
  • 2. To perform statutory duties;
  • 3. To respond to public health emergencies or protect life/property in emergencies;
  • 4. For public interest activities (e.g., news reporting, public oversight);
  • 5. Processing publicly available Personal Information within legal bounds;
  • 6. Other scenarios permitted by law.

If third parties provide your Personal Information, we ensure they comply with legal requirements and obtain your consent.

2. Use of Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies are industry standards. To enable service functionality, we store Cookies or LocalStorage on your device, containing identifiers, service names, and usage statistics.

We do not use Cookies for purposes beyond this Policy. You may block or delete Cookies via device settings, but this may affect service availability.
